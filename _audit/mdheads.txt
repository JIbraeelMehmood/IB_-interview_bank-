  12| 4. **Prevent:** dobara na ho iske liye kya lagayenge? (idempotency, unique constraint, timeout, test)

  22| - `pm = static`: workers ki fixed tadaad, predictable, high-traffic ke liye achha.
  23| - `pm = dynamic`: `pm.max_children`, `pm.start_servers`, `pm.min_spare_servers`, `pm.max_spare_servers` ke beech workers ghat-barhte hain.
  24| - `pm = ondemand`: jab request aaye tab worker banta hai, kam traffic wali sites ke liye.
  25| - `pm.max_requests`: worker itni requests ke baad restart hota hai (memory leaks se bachne ke liye).
